# The 1 October 2026 cutover — the books begin

**The owner, 15 September 2026:** *"books start on 10/01 but we need to do a complete dry run this month to troubleshoot
EVERYTHING and make sure everything works starting 10/01"*.

This is what has to change on 1 October, measured before anything was built. It sits on branch `work/cutover`, which
is **not merged before 1 October**: a migration runs on the next start after it is merged, and this one would take
the dry run's own September money out of every report.

## What the branch holds so far

- `books-start.ts`: `SITE_STARTS_ON` = `2026-10-01`.
- Migration `0123_october_books_start.sql`, additive: the same rule as 0114, by the day the money arrived.
  - `claim_payments` with a received date before 1 Oct → out of books;
  - `cash_receipts` with a month before 2026-10 → out of books;
  - `remittance_holdbacks` with a received date before 1 Oct → out of books.

  Not changed: `claim_imports` (flagged by provenance: September's daily reports were real, and a fill filled on
  30 Sept and collected on 2 Oct is October revenue), and `expenses` (which carry no flag).

## What that shows has to change as well, before 1 October

**C-1. The cash P&L for a month before the books counts costs with no revenue.**
OBSERVATION: after the re-flag, September's cash revenue is $0.00 but its costs (wholesaler invoices paid,
$136,551.40, and bills paid, $507.33) are still counted: a −$137,058.73 month.
SHOULD BE: a month before the books begin is not an account. It is either refused, as the AR report refuses it, or
shown as the dry run with revenue and costs on the same footing. Costs without revenue is the phantom that 0114 was
written to end for August.
DIFFERENCE: yes. `profit-and-loss.ts` (A's) needs to refuse or label months before `SITE_STARTS_ON`, as
`monthIsReportable` does for AR. The same holds for September accrual, which after the cutover still shows as if it
were in the books.

**C-2. The payer page bills the dry run's claims and none of its payments.**
OBSERVATION: `/payers/owed` loads the last 400 days of fills with no books-start filter, while payments before the books
are excluded. After the cutover it shows September's $253,986.08 billed, $0.00 received and $254,045.08 outstanding.
SHOULD BE: receivables and payments are held to the same boundary. The AR report already filters receivables by fill
date (`receivablesAsAt`).
DIFFERENCE: yes. `owedNow` needs the same boundary, unless the owner decides September's unpaid claims are opening
receivables (question 2), in which case both sides need it, not neither.

**C-3. Thirteen tests are written to the September 1 date.**
- `ar-report.test.ts` ×6
- `books-start.test.ts` ×3
- `books-start-fill-date.test.ts` ×2
- `copay-remit.test.ts` ×1
- `test-pull.test.ts` ×1

They move a month, or better, read `SITE_STARTS_ON`, in the same commit as the constant.

## The questions only the owner and his accountant can answer

1. **Opening bank balance on 1 October:** the Emprise statement's closing balance for 30 September?
2. **September fills paid in October.**
   - **As opening receivables:** September's unpaid claims carry into the October books, and October's payments
     settle them. Needs C-2 in its carry-over form, and a way to mark September claims as opening balances.
   - **As October cash only:** that money is October cash and settles nothing; September's claims appear on no
     October report. This is the migration as built.
3. **Month-end documents for September** (bank statement, card statement, Veridikal, rebate) arrive in early October.
   Run them against the dry run in early October, or rely on August's?
4. **Keep a record of the dry run.** After the cutover, September's reports are refused (AR) or read as not the books
   (P&L). Print or export September's AR report and both P&L bases on 30 September, if they are wanted for the record.
